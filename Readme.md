
# OmniFood Responsive Food Delivery Landing Page

## Project Overview

OmniFood is a single-page food delivery landing page developed as a front-end assignment. The website promotes healthy food delivery and follows the layout shown in the assignment reference images.

It includes a hero section, How It Works section, city cards, customer testimonials, pricing plans, and a footer.

## Website Sections

### 1. Header and Hero
- OmniFood logo and navigation links.
- Large headline with a dark background overlay.
- "I'm Hungry" and "Show me more" buttons.

### 2. How It Works
- Phone mockup displaying a food image.
- Three numbered steps.
- Google Play and App Store buttons.

### 3. Our Cities
Four city cards:
- Lisbon
- London
- Berlin
- San Francisco

Each card contains an image, city name, customer and chef details, and a profile link.

### 4. Customer Testimonials
- Three customer quotes.
- Customer profile pictures and names.
- Dark overlay over a background image to improve readability.

### 5. Pricing Plans
- Premium plan: $399 per month.
- Pro plan: $199 per month.
- Starter plan: $19 per meal.
- Each card includes a feature list and signup button.

### 6. Footer
- About, Blog, Press, iOS App, and Android App links.
- Social media placeholders.
- Copyright information.

## Responsive Design

The website uses Flexbox, CSS Grid, and media queries to adjust its layout for different screen sizes.

- Desktop: Four city cards and three pricing cards appear in columns.
- Tablet: City cards are arranged in two columns.
- Mobile: The How It Works section and testimonials stack vertically, and pricing cards appear in one column.
- Small mobile screens: City cards also stack in one column.

The main content sections are designed to occupy approximately one viewport in height, while allowing extra height when the content needs more space.
